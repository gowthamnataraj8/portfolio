Featured real project

Impact of Cost Structure and Debt Strategy on Corporate Profitability

Corporate profitability study using 10 years of SEC XBRL financial filings. I extracted and cleaned 21,087 firm-year observations across 2,843 companies, engineered financial ratios (ROA, debt ratio, cost structure), and compared a fixed effects panel regression model against a Random Forest regressor to identify the strongest drivers of profitability.
